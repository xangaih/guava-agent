# Harbor Mutual Insurance — Policy FAQ

## What is my deductible?
Standard homeowners policies carry a $1,000 deductible. Auto policies carry a
$500 deductible for collision and a $250 deductible for comprehensive claims.
Your exact deductible is listed on your declarations page.

## How do I file a claim?
You can file a claim by calling this line any time, day or night, or by
logging into the Harbor Mutual online portal. You'll need your policy number,
the date of the incident, and a description of what happened. Most claims
receive an initial response within one business day.

## Is water damage covered?
Sudden and accidental water damage — such as a burst pipe — is covered under
standard homeowners policies. Gradual damage from long-term leaks, and
flooding from an external body of water, is not covered and requires separate
flood insurance.

## Is my property covered if it's stolen from my car?
Personal property stolen from a vehicle is typically covered under your
homeowners or renters policy's personal property section, not your auto
policy, subject to your deductible and coverage limits.

## How long does a claim take to process?
Most straightforward claims are resolved within 5-10 business days after all
documentation is submitted. Complex claims involving an adjuster site visit
can take 2-4 weeks.

## What is not covered by my policy?
Common exclusions include flooding, earth movement (earthquakes, sinkholes),
wear and tear, neglect, and intentional damage. Business-related losses at a
home address usually require a separate business policy rider.

## Can I add a driver to my auto policy?
Yes. Call this line or use the online portal to add a driver. You'll need
their full name, date of birth, and driver's license number. Rate changes
take effect immediately upon adding a driver.

## What are your customer service hours?
Our contact center is open Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern, and
Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern. Claims can be filed 24/7 through this line or
the online portal.

## How do I get a copy of my policy documents?
Policy documents, including your declarations page and full policy contract,
are available anytime in the online portal under "My Documents," or you can
request a copy be mailed to you.

## Will filing a claim raise my premium?
It depends on the type and size of the claim, and your claims history over
the past several years. A single small claim rarely causes a significant
increase; multiple claims in a short period are more likely to affect your
renewal rate.

## Do you offer roadside assistance?
Roadside assistance is available as an optional add-on to auto policies. If
you have it, it's listed on your declarations page along with the number to
call for towing, jump-starts, and lockout service.

## How do I cancel my policy?
You can cancel by calling this line and speaking with a representative, or by
submitting a cancellation request through the online portal. Refunds for
prepaid premium are prorated based on your cancellation date.
